# NPA — No Phones Around (Android)

An interactive digital wellness & anti-doomscrolling campaign Android app built with **Kotlin** and **Jetpack Compose** (Material 3 + neo-brutalist playful styling).

## Features
- **Beranda (Home)**:
  - **Quote Strip** carousel (top & bottom)
  - **Drop The Phone Hero** interactive 6-character table scene (Before vs After states with Nopi the Otter mascot)
  - **Indonesia Digital Stats** cards & **Data yang Bicara** screen time bar chart + live NPA mode counter
  - **Education Interactive Lab** (1 Jam Scroll vs 1 Jam Ngobrol, Brain Overload tap-to-explode tabs, Empathy Phone-on-Table toggle, Moment Quality 20%→88% simulation)
  - **4 Daily Habits (Tips)**, **Myths vs Facts** flip/cycle cards, **Moments**, and **NPA: The Board Game** teaser
- **Tes & NPA Lab**:
  - **Doomscrolling Test (`FeedApp™`)**: Auto-scrolling simulated social feed with awareness timer and Nopi-guided tier results
  - **Focus Shield**: 25-second notification temptation resistance game with real-time character reactions
  - **Presence Meter**: 30-second presence-maintenance challenge
  - **Connection Story** & **Storytelling ("Pilih jalanmu")**: Branching dinner narratives
- **Challenge & Pledge Card**:
  - 1-Day, 3-Day, and 7-Day NPA challenges with personalized Pledge Card persisted in DataStore and shareable via Android Share Sheet
- **Digital Detox Timer**:
  - 6-stage growth stopwatch (`🌱 -> 🌿 -> 🌳 -> 🌳🐦 -> 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 -> ☀️`) with DataStore-persisted best time & total sessions
- **Tim NPA**:
  - 8 creator profiles with photos, role pills, bios, social handles, and tap-to-wave reactions
- **Floating Companions**:
  - Interactive **OtterPet (Nopi)** with 6 reactive moods and periodic blinking
  - **Music Player** with 4 ambient tracks played in-app and optional YouTube deep-linking
